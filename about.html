About The Coastal Table

Fresh Food. Good Coffee. Made for Every Journey.

The Coastal Table is a fictional café and restaurant created for this prototype project. It represents a premium but welcoming Australian coastal café — the kind of place locals return to and travellers are glad they stopped.

The experience is built around a simple journey: discover what’s available, order ahead if you like, visit, pay at the counter, and collect. There is no online payment. Everything is designed so you know whether we’re open, what’s on today, and what you can get quickly.

How ordering works

  1. Browse the menu and today’s specials
  2. Add items to your order
  3. Enter your name and mobile number
  4. Place the order online
  5. Come to the restaurant, pay at the counter, and collect

Service periods

The kitchen runs from 6:00 AM to 7:30 PM (Australia/Hobart). Breakfast is served from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. The afternoon menu runs from 12:00 PM until close. Cabinet food, coffee and tea are available throughout kitchen hours. Individual dish availability can differ from the featured service period.

This prototype

This website is a frontend prototype suitable for GitHub Pages. Orders are stored in your browser (localStorage) for demonstration. Menu data, hours and specials are configured in JavaScript files so they can be edited without touching the layout. Real photography can be dropped into the images/ folders when available.

Future versions can connect to a backend, kitchen display, inventory and notifications — the structure is intentionally ready for that.
